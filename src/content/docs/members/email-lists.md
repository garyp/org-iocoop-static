IO Coöperative has a handful of different mailing lists provided via Google Groups.

- “[iocoop-members](https://groups.google.com/g/iocoop-members)” : This list is for general
  discussion within the membership and co-op announcements. Any member can post to the list. All
  members are joined to this list by default.
- “[iocoop-announce](https://groups.google.com/g/iocoop-announce)” : This list is an alternative to
  iocoop-members, will only receive co-op announcements, and can only be posted to by the board or
  designated working groups. If you don’t want to receive emails from other members,
  [set your iocoop-members “Subscription” to “No email”](https://groups.google.com/g/iocoop-members/membership)
  and
  [click “Ask to join group” in the iocoop-announce group](https://groups.google.com/g/iocoop-announce/%20).
  If you are a co-op member you need to be subscribed to one of these two Google Groups.
- “[iocoop-board](https://groups.google.com/g/iocoop-board)” : The board of directors uses this list
  to communicate
- “[iocoop-ops](https://groups.google.com/g/iocoop-ops)” : The operations team (co-op members with
  administrative access to all of the co-op infrastructure) use this list to communicate
