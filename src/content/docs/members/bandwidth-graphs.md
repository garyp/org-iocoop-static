<https://grafana.scl.iocoop.org/>

- Determine what PDU port or ethernet switch port your hardware is plugged into by logging in to the
  [IO Coop member portal](https://iocoop.org/members/) using the email address you used to join the
  coop.
- Click “System Information” and you should see details about any/all servers that you have
  colocated. If you don’t see your server or something’s wrong, email [support](/support/) to let us
  know.
- Next, head over to the monitoring server : <https://grafana.scl.iocoop.org/>
- Log in with your Google account. This could be your Gmail email address, your Google
  Apps/GSuite/Workspace email address, or your non-Google email address that you’ve setup a Google
  account with. If doing so doesn’t get you access, it’s because we don’t have that email address in
  our system, please email [support](/support/) to let us know.
- Navigate to the switch or PDU that your system is plugged into and the port that belongs to you.
