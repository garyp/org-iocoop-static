## Responsibilities

The Operations committee is responsible for operating, maintaining, and monitoring the coöperative’s
computing infrastructure, and provides technical support to members for services the coöperative
provides. Specific responsibilities include, but are not limited to:

- Monitoring power and bandwidth usage
- Maintaining VPS clusters
- Maintaining authentication (Google)
- Maintaining DNS
- [Automation of infrastructure provisioning and configuration](https://github.com/iocoop/configs)
- [Maintaining the Minio Restic backups of IO Coöperative infrastructure](https://github.com/iocoop/members/wiki/Backups)
- Communicating to the membership about service outages
- [Maintenance of Grafana monitoring](https://github.com/iocoop/members/wiki/Monitoring)
- Providing support via Matrix and the ticketing system (support.iocoop.org)
- Granting and revoking access to coöperative systems and network infrastructure

## Authority & Limitations

The Operations Committee is authorized to develop policies and procedures for meeting its functional
responsibilities, and delegating tasks among its members. The decisions, policies, and procedures of
the committee may be overturned by majority vote of the board or a super-majority vote of the
membership at large.

The chair of the committee is authorized to act as an agent of the coöperative, with respect to
facilities and Internet service vendors, when acting within the good faith, authorization, and
knowledge of the board of directors.

## Standing Agenda Items

- Review high-priority outstanding tickets and assign someone to take them on
- Identify areas for improvements to monitoring, automation, and operations
