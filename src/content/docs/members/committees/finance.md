## Responsibilities

The finance committee is responsible for the financial well being of the coöperative. The specific
tasks include:

- Paying datacenter bills. Currently
  [they auto-pay](https://github.com/iocoop/members/wiki/How-to-pay-a-Hurricane-Electric-HE.net-or-Honeycomb-Datacenter-Bill)
  so this only involves record keeping.
- Checking non-standard invoices we receive from our vendors to ensure they’re warranted and we
  should pay them. Contesting the charge if they aren’t through support tickets or emails with the
  vendor.
- [Paying City, State and Federal taxes](https://github.com/iocoop/members/wiki/Taxes)
- [Paying annual ARIN fees](https://github.com/iocoop/members/wiki/ARIN-IP-Address-and-AS-Autonomous-System-allocation-account)
- [Updating recurring payments when our debit cards change numbers or expire](https://github.com/iocoop/members/wiki/Recurring-IO-Coop-Payments-Paid-By-SF-Fire-Debit-Card)

## Collecting Revenue

- [Dealing with members who don’t pay their bills](https://github.com/iocoop/members/wiki/How-to-deal-with-a-delinquent-member%27s-Freshbooks-account)
- Pursuing members who’s autobill credit cards stop working
- Resolving tickets in the [finance ticket queue](https://github.com/iocoop/finance/)
- Dealing with billing complaints/issues or contested invoices from members that stem from
  Freshbooks invoices or reminders
- Issuing invoices for members with bandwidth overages or who request services directly from the
  datacenter and incur costs

## Producing Reports

- [End-of-fiscal-year accounting](https://github.com/iocoop/accounting/blob/master/README.md#generating-the-annual-report)
  (Fiscal Year ends [June 30th](https://github.com/iocoop/members/wiki/Taxes#tax-year)) (w/
  [accountant](https://github.com/iocoop/members/wiki/Taxes#how-to-prepare-irs-and-ca-ftb-tax-returns))
- Prepare financial statements
- Review financials
- [Reconcile financials](https://github.com/iocoop/members/wiki/How-to-reconcile-transactions-and-perform-bookkeeping-with-Beancount)
  (basic bookkeeping)

## Authority and Limitations

The Administrative & Legal Committee is authorized to develop policies and procedures for meeting
its functional responsibilities, and delegating tasks among its members. The decisions, policies,
and procedures of the committee may be overturned by majority vote of the board or a super-majority
vote of the membership at large.

The chair of the committee is authorized to act as an agent of the coöperative, with respect to
legal and administrative vendors, when acting within the good faith, authorization, and knowledge of
the board of directors.
