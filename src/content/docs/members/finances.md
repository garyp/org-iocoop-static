## What do our fees go towards?

The majority of the fees we collect for VPSs and colocation go directly to the datacenters and
transit providers that provide us the space, power and bandwidth to get your machines up and
connected to the internet. We set our prices to target our datacenter and transit costs plus 20%.

## What does the 20% go towards?

The 20% on top of our datacenter and transit costs which makeup our prices pays for :

- [PDUs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Power_distribution_unit),
  [ATSs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transfer_switch) and power cabling
- Network infrastructure like our routers, switches and copper and fiber network cabling
- VPS server hardware
- Credit card and Paypal processing fees
- Financial services like Freshbooks which we invoice and collect money through
- Our tax preparer
- Legal counsel
- Communication services such as our phone number, mailing address and GitHub
- Domain names
- City State and Federal taxes and licensing fees
- Insurance

The 20% **does not** pay for :

- Anyone’s time. We are member supported and the operation of the co-op is done wholly by the
  members (with the exception of our tax prep)

Per our [bylaws](/docs/policies/bylaws/#section-1002-books-and-records), any member may inspect our
accounting books and records.
