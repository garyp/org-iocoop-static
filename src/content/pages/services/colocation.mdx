Datacenter colocation is one of the two primary services IO Coöperative provides. Our members
colocate hardware in our datacenters to run their businesses, support their non-profits, and host
their personal projects.

Since IO Coöperative is a consumer coöperative, it’s owned by the members. As a result, our prices
are derived directly from our costs for power, space and bandwidth
[plus whatever overhead is needed for the infrastructure](/docs/members/finances/).

## Request colocation

To request colocation, send an email to [support@iocoop.org](mailto:support@iocoop.org) with the
details below. If you have a request that is not easily handled this way, feel free to say so in the
same email. Not a member yet? [Join here!](https://iocoop.org/join/)

<Fields>
  <Field name="Member email">Your IO Coöperative member email address.</Field>
  <Field name="Hostname">
    Used for reverse DNS, so it needs to be fully qualified. Please label your server with its
    hostname on the front and back.
  </Field>
  <Field name="Server height">
    In [rack units](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rack_unit), up to 8U. Your server must have rails
    to mount it in the cabinet.
  </Field>
  <Field name="Server depth">In inches. The server must be less than 28.75 inches deep.</Field>
  <Field name="Power consumption">
    Estimated, in watts. Your server must accept 208 volt power. You can measure it with a
    [Kill-a-watt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kill_A_Watt).
  </Field>
  <Field name="Power supplies">
    The number of [power supplies](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Power_supply_unit_(computer)>)
    with [power sockets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IEC_60320#C14).
  </Field>
  <Field name="Socket orientation">
    As viewed from the back of the server: 1 on the left, 1 on the right, 2 on the left, 2 on the
    right, one on each side, or other.
  </Field>
  <Field name="Bandwidth">
    Estimated, measured at
    [95th percentile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burstable_billing#95th_percentile) in Mbps.
  </Field>
  <Field name="Public IPv4 addresses">
    The number you will use within the first month. Additional IPs can be assigned later when
    needed.
  </Field>
  <Field name="Public network ports">This is almost always 1.</Field>
  <Field name="Private network ports">
    Needed for [IPMI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intelligent_Platform_Management_Interface) or if
    you have multiple servers in the cabinet which need to be connected together.
  </Field>
  <Field name="Brand and model">The make and model of your server.</Field>
  <Field name="Comments">Anything else we should know.</Field>
</Fields>

<Callout title="More than one IPv4 address" type="warning">
  Any more than 1 public IPv4 address requires written
  [justification](https://www.arin.net/resources/guide/request/documentation/#requests-requiring-demonstration-of-utilization-of-end-user-assignments-by-isps)
  that we can provide to [ARIN](https://www.arin.net/).
</Callout>

## Our facilities

Our virtual private server and colocation services are hosted at the Hurricane Electric FMT2
datacenter at
[48233 Warm Springs Blvd, Fremont, California, USA](https://www.google.com/maps/place/48233%20Warm%20Springs%20Blvd,%20Fremont,%20CA%2094539).

If you don’t live near the datacenter such that you’ll need to ship your server, here are
instructions on [how to ship a machine for installation](/docs/members/rack-a-machine/).

We also offer [virtual servers](/services/virtual-private-servers/) at all datacenters.
