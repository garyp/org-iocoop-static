A [virtual private server](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virtual_private_server) (VPS) is a
virtualized server instance that runs alongside others on the same physical machine. Since dedicated
server hardware often remains underutilized, virtualization maximizes resource use — both
economically and environmentally — by allowing multiple servers to share the same infrastructure. It
is conceptually similar to an AWS EC2 instance or GCP Compute Engine VM.

A VPS is an economical and efficient use of resources in which you get full administrative access
without the hassle, expense and responsibility of maintaining physical gear.

We offer VPS hosting to co-op members on collaboratively managed servers. Our infrastructure is
powered by the [Ganeti](https://ganeti.org/) virtual machine management system, ensuring high
reliability and performance. The VPS service is managed by a dedicated team of experienced co-op
members.

## Frequently asked questions

<Faq>
  <FaqItem question="What’s the maximum supported config?">
    The largest single instance we support is 8 slices.
  </FaqItem>
  <FaqItem question="Can I purchase a bunch of extra disk space for my one-slice VPS?">
    No. Disk allocation is limited to two times the number of slices. For example, 100GB of disk
    would require two slices and two extra disk units.
  </FaqItem>
  <FaqItem question="What do I upload for the “SSH Public Key”?">
    Find the `id_ed25519.pub` or `id_rsa.pub` file created [when you created your SSH key
    pair](https://docs.github.com/en/authentication/connecting-to-github-with-ssh/generating-a-new-ssh-key-and-adding-it-to-the-ssh-agent)
    and attach it.
  </FaqItem>
</Faq>

We also offer [colocation](/services/colocation/) for members who want to run their own hardware.
