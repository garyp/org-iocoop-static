## Mission

IO Coöperative provides its members with technology infrastructure to sustain and enhance their
professional, academic, and personal lives. We accomplish this as a member-owned and run California
[Consumer Coöperative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Consumer_cooperative).

Our members do all the work of running the co-op, from the infrastructure to the accounting.

Due to this shared responsibility of administration and operation, the members of the coöperative
need to be able to know and trust each other. As a result of this need for mutual understanding and
trust, some people and organizations will not be a good fit as members of the coöperative, and as a
result will not be able to join.

Given that there are no resources to administer and operate the coöperative other than the members
themselves, all members are expected contribute to the administration and operation of the
coöperative.

## Services we provide

IO Coöperative provides server co-location, network connectivity, virtual hosting, and other
technology products and services to its members.

## What do we mean by a coöperative?

IO Coöperative exists to offer services to our members. The money collected from members for those
services goes to pay for the costs to run the co-op. These costs comprise things like the rent to
the datacenters, network transit, insurance premiums, merchant fees, taxes, billing services,
network hardware, cabling, etc. As our costs change over time, member pricing changes as well,
because our service prices are based on our costs.

There are no paid employees. All the labor to operate the co-op is provided by its members.
Crucially, this means that unlike a traditional ISP where you pay money and they provide service,
with the coöperative, in addition to paying for services, our members also need to help run the
co-op, since they’re the only ones who can. This assistance could be through technical operations,
serving on the board of directors, financial bookkeeping, fielding member support requests, helping
new members get setup, installing new servers in datacenters and other activities.

When you, as a member, need technical support, those requests may take longer to service than with
other ISPs that have employees providing 24/7 support. We’re just a group of people with a common
interest and a shared goal.

If you’re a member of the co-op, [contact us](/contact/) to find out how you can help. Technical
aptitude is not required as many of the tasks to run the co-op aren’t technical in nature.

If you’re not a member, but the co-op sounds like something you’d be interested in,
[join IO Coöperative here](/join/).
